The market guides the outcome

Our analysis provides an informed starting point, but no analysis can predict the exact rent or leasing timeline. We listen to the market through inquiries, showings and applications, then adjust accordingly. Our priority is effective rental income: what your property actually earns after vacancy and concessions. A higher asking price is only worthwhile if the additional rent outweighs the income lost while waiting.

A realistic starting point

The home offers meaningful advantages: approximately 3,301 square feet, four bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms, a three-car garage and a flexible Ashby Grand layout in a newer gated community. Those features support positioning above smaller leased homes. They do not, however, establish that renters will pay the $3,900-$4,500 asking prices seen elsewhere in the search.

Our recommended launch is $3,495 per month. This balances the home's strengths against recent lease results and newer competing homes. A working range of $3,395-$3,595 reflects the uncertainty in this limited sample; the upper end is a test price, not the preferred strategy when minimizing vacancy is the priority.

What the evidence actually supports

The supplied CMA contains three leased homes at $3,000, $3,100 and $3,450, and seven available homes asking $3,400-$4,500. The most relevant active price signals are a similarly sized home at $3,400 and a brand-new five-bedroom home recently reduced to $3,600. Higher asking prices remain unproven until a lease is secured.

A leasing target, not a guarantee

Target a signed lease within 14-21 days once the home is vacant, fully show-ready, professionally marketed and readily accessible. This is an operating objective, not a timing forecast established by three leases. The actual rent-start date may be later because of move-in scheduling or HOA approval. If the home remains occupied, set a firm availability date and showing plan before launch.

01Why these communities were selected+

01 | SELECTING THE RIGHT COMPETITION

Location alone is not enough

Why Bella Collina is excluded

Although Bella Collina is nearby, its private-club setting, golf course, dining, spa, fitness and resort recreation create a materially different rental offering. That lifestyle can support a premium, depending on the home and the membership privileges included. Treating those rents as directly comparable would risk overstating Willow Ridge's achievable rent.

Bella Collina is therefore excluded from the primary pricing evidence. No arbitrary dollar deduction has been applied to its rents. Membership eligibility, fees and tenant access vary; proximity alone does not make the properties interchangeable.

Willow Ridge has its own appeal

Pulte advertises gated access, a community pool and cabana, a fire pit area, natural gas and rolling-hill homesites. Its website also advertises one-gig internet and streaming TV. Confirm the current HOA package and tenant access before promising those services in the rental listing.

How this analysis handles the missing neighborhood comps

No Willow Ridge lease appears in the supplied CMA. The analysis uses nearby Minneola homes as substitutes, emphasizing similar size, modern condition, layout, garage capacity and community recreation. These are imperfect substitutes: the sample does not isolate a reliable Montverde-versus-Minneola location premium.

04Pricing plan and the cost of vacancy+

04 | PRICING AND EXECUTION

Protect annual rental income

Launch at $3,495; make the first two weeks count

Before launch: complete the walkthrough, confirm repairs and cleaning, agree on the possession date, arrange photography, and verify HOA rental rules, approval timing and included services. Market only with a dependable showing plan.

Day 5: review qualified inquiries, completed tours, feedback and applications. Check presentation, access and availability before concluding that price is the only issue.

Days 10-14: if exposure and access are adequate but qualified interest is weak, recommend $3,395 rather than allowing the listing to drift. Strong interest may justify holding briefly; no price is a guaranteed floor.

Day 21: if no lease is secured, reassess current competition and unresolved objections promptly. Keep screening standards consistent; use price and condition to improve appeal.
